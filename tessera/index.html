Tessera Wilds

An endless world of blocks. Dig, farm, build, fight.

Controls

  • W A S D move Space jump Shift sprint
  • Left click dig or attack
  • Right click place, use, till, plant, eat
  • 1–9 or wheel: pick a hotbar slot
  • E inventory Esc pause F3 details
  • Mouse or Arrow keys look around

Getting started

  • You start with a Maker Table and a set of seeds.
  • Place the table, then right-click it: every tool, weapon, armour piece and building block is made on its 3 × 3 grid.
  • Punch a tree for logs. Logs make planks, planks make sticks and your first Pick, Spade and Tiller.
